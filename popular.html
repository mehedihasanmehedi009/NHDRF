Plant Care Guide

☀️ Light Requirements

Monstera grows best in bright, indirect sunlight. Avoid harsh direct sun.

💧 Watering

Water when the top 1–2 inches of soil feel dry. Do not let the soil remain waterlogged.

🐾 Pet Safety

Monstera is toxic to cats and dogs if ingested. Keep it away from pets.

🪴 Repotting

Repot every 1–2 years, or when the roots outgrow the existing container.
